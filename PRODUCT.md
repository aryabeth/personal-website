## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, weighted equally:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** evaluating Arya for an employed web developer role. They skim fast, often on a phone, and need to see seniority, stack, and proof of production work before taking the resume into their pipeline.
- **Freelance clients** (businesses and agencies) considering Arya for WordPress, Shopify, Next.js or Webflow builds and ongoing site maintenance. They want evidence of real, live client sites and an easy way to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Arya Beta Widyatmika, a Bali-based web developer (www.aryabeth.com). It exists to turn a visit into a hiring or project conversation.

Success means a visitor does one or more of:

- downloads the resume (`/resume.pdf`);
- starts a direct conversation over WhatsApp or email;
- opens live client sites to judge the quality of real work;
- connects on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

Ten years of shipping and maintaining production websites for digital agencies and in-house teams, not just building them once. Arya has kept a portfolio of 20+ live sites stable and up to date, turns Figma and Adobe XD designs into responsive builds, and communicates clearly with designers, clients and non-technical stakeholders. ## Product Principles

1. Proof over claims: real live sites, screenshots and specific roles beat adjectives.
2. Every visitor is one step from action: resume, WhatsApp/email and LinkedIn stay reachable from anywhere on the page.
3. Serve both audiences without forking: recruiters scan for seniority and stack, clients scan for live work; the same content must answer both quickly.
4. Honest credit: always separate what Arya built from what collaborators designed or managed.
5. The site itself is a work sample: it must be fast, responsive and well built.
